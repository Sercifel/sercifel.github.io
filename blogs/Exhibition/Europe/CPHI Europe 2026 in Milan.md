# CPHI Europe 2026 in Milan: Global Pharma Supply Chain Returns to Fiera Milano in October

## Event Overview
![](https://knect365.imgix.net/uploads/cphi-milan-logo-30cf1628f05ff9ac0b8343785edd73ae.webp)

[CPHI Europe 2026 in Milan](https://www.cphi.com/europe/) takes place October 6–8, 2026 at Fiera Milano, Italy, and is positioned as a major global meeting point for the pharmaceutical supply chain. The official CPHI Milan site frames the event as a place where the full pharma ecosystem connects, with established zones spanning APIs, bioproduction, contract services, CROs, excipients, finished dosage, machinery and equipment, and packaging and drug delivery. The 2026 edition also introduces new zones for AI & Tech, contamination control, cold chain and logistics, and labelling, indicating a broader focus that links manufacturing, data, and compliance. For international buyers and suppliers, this mix signals a trade fair that goes beyond ingredient sourcing into manufacturing, packaging, and operational technologies that increasingly shape product quality and speed to market.

The 2026 structure emphasizes a full‑supply‑chain layout with dedicated zones and a multi‑track conference program. CPHI’s site highlights the event’s 2025 scale as context for the 2026 edition, reporting tens of thousands of attendees, thousands of exhibiting companies, and broad global representation. While specific 2026 exhibitor lists are published separately, the show positions itself as a central platform to connect with suppliers and partners across pharma, including machinery and packaging stakeholders. The addition of new zones such as AI & Tech and Labelling shows a growing focus on digitalization, traceability, and regulatory compliance, which are increasingly critical for pharmaceutical operations. For attendees, these zones provide clearer pathways to compare technology offerings alongside traditional sourcing categories.

CPHI Milan pairs its exhibition with extensive conference programming, aimed at connecting commercial sourcing with regulatory, technical, and operational insights.
