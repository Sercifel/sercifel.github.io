# Inside Kaohsiung Industrial Automation Exhibition 2027: Smart Manufacturing, Robotics, and More

![](https://i0.wp.com/censtrade.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/%E9%AB%98%E9%9B%84%E8%87%AA%E5%8B%95%E5%8C%96%E5%B1%95LOGO.webp?fit=300%2C226&ssl=1)
The **[Kaohsiung Industrial Automation Exhibition 2027 (KIAE 2027)](https://censtrade.com/kaohsiung-automation/)** will take place from **May 26 to May 29, 2027**, at the **Kaohsiung Exhibition Center in Kaohsiung, Taiwan**. Organized by the Economic Daily News, the four-day exhibition focuses on industrial automation, smart manufacturing, smart machinery, artificial intelligence, robotics, and industrial control.

For those following manufacturing technology in Taiwan, the exhibition offers a concentrated look at equipment, components, software, and automation technologies serving modern production environments.

## What Is the Kaohsiung Industrial Automation Exhibition?

KIAE is an industrial exhibition focused on automation and manufacturing technologies, with particular attention to the manufacturing market in southern Taiwan.

Rather than concentrating on one type of machinery, the exhibition covers technologies used across different parts of manufacturing, from machine tools and production equipment to control, sensing, inspection, material handling, and factory digitalization.

### When and Where Is KIAE 2027?

The confirmed event details are:

- **Dates:** May 26–29, 2027
- **Venue:** Kaohsiung Exhibition Center
- **Address:** No. 39, Chenggong 2nd Road, Qianzhen District, Kaohsiung City, Taiwan
- **Organizer:** Economic Daily News

The exhibition will run from Wednesday through Saturday.

## What Will Be Featured at KIAE 2027?

The announced exhibition scope covers a broad range of technologies associated with industrial production and automation, including:

- Industrial automation equipment and control systems
- Industrial and collaborative robots
- Machine vision
- Smart manufacturing and AI applications
- Machine tools and smart machinery
- Sensors, inspection, and industrial measurement
- Motors, transmission, pneumatic, and hydraulic equipment
- Industrial Internet of Things and industrial software
- Automated warehousing, logistics, and material handling systems
- Electrical and electronic automation components

The mix of categories reflects different layers of factory automation, from individual components and machines to integrated production and control technologies.
