    The "Candlelight Weaving Tower": Where 5-Axis Precision Meets Geometric Artistry at the Victor Taichung CNC Skill Competition

    Jul 08, 2026

    In the world of advanced manufacturing, the line between engineering and art is becoming increasingly blurred. Nowhere was this more evident than in the recent Victor Taichung CNC Multi-Axis Skill Competition, where the challenge wasn't just about machining a part—it was about crafting a masterpiece. The competition's centerpiece, a stunning work titled the "Candlelight Weaving Tower" (燭光織影塔), perfectly encapsulates this new era of intelligent, artisanal manufacturing.

    Bridging Education and Industry

    The Victor Taichung Cup CNC Multi-Axis Skill Competition is designed with a clear and vital mission: to elevate the importance of multi-axis machining technology within universities and training institutions. By strengthening the connection between teaching practice and talent cultivation, the competition encourages learners to deepen their multi-axis operation and application capabilities, building a solid technical foundation for the machine tool industry. More importantly, it aims to connect industry, government, and academia to form a sustainable and stable talent development chain, collectively promoting industrial technology upgrades and development.

    The Design Philosophy: A Fusion of Geometry and Architecture

    The 2026 competition challenge was built around a design that takes contemporary geometric aesthetics as its core, cleverly integrating elements of classical architecture. The "Candlelight Weaving Tower" is a testament to what's possible when complex geometry meets high-precision machining.

    • A Flowing Spiral Column: The work uses a streamlined spiral column to create a distinct vertical tension, showcasing a mechanical beauty where strength and elegance coexist.
    • Symbolic Rectangular Grids: Symbolizing the trajectory of time, rectangular window grids are incorporated to create interlocking spatial layers of reality and illusion.
    • A Diamond-Cut Apex: The piece is crowned with a diamond-cut facet, condensing light refraction and the passage of time into the very structure of the work.

    Through this design, CNC machining is no longer just a manufacturing process; it becomes a creative journey that blends aesthetics and innovation, infusing mechanical craftsmanship with more artistry and imaginative space.

    To fully appreciate how the intricate spiral columns and diamond-cut apex transition from digital blueprint to physical reality, we invite you to experience the complete five-axis machining showcase on YouTube. The footage captures the Vcenter-AX380 in its element—spindle whirring at high speed, the A/C axis tilting and rotating the workpiece with fluid dexterity, and shimmering steel ribbons peeling away to reveal the delicate latticework. It is a mesmerizing visual narrative that proves CNC machining is as much about choreography as it is about cutting.

    The Machine Behind the Masterpiece: Vcenter-AX380

    Bringing such an intricate design to life requires a machine tool of exceptional capability. The competition utilized Victor Taichung's Vcenter-AX380, a high-precision 5-axis machining center that is as robust as it is accurate. With its fixed column C-frame structure, the Vcenter-AX380 is built for high rigidity and heavy cutting, making it ideal for demanding applications. Key specifications include:

    • Travel: X: 700mm, Y: 500mm, Z: 540mm
    • A/C Axis Rotary Table: Featuring an A-axis tilt range of -120° to 30° and a C-axis for 360° continuous rotation
    • Table Work Area: Ø380mm with a maximum load capacity of 200kg
    • Spindle: BBT-40 taper with a maximum speed of 12,000 rpm
    • Accuracy: Positioning accuracy of 0.010mm and repeatability of 0.007mm (±0.0035mm)

    As the video of the machining process shows, the Vcenter-AX380's high-degree-of-freedom machining capability allows it to precisely handle complex curved surfaces and high-density open structures, ensuring that every delicate geometric detail of the design is fully realized.

    A Symphony of Collaboration

    The success of this project is a testament to the power of collaboration within Taiwan's machine tool ecosystem. Victor Taichung brought together an all-star team of technology partners to ensure the highest quality result:

    • Controller: Siemens SINUMERIK ONE – The digital-native core provided the intelligence for high-precision tool center point (TCP) control.
    • CAM Software: hyperMILL by OPEN MIND – Enabled advanced collision avoidance and full machine simulation, bridging the gap between virtual programming and real-world production.
    • Cutting Fluid: Blaser Swisslube – Provided sustainable lubrication and effective vibration damping for a stable and safe cutting process.
    • Tool Holder: SYIC – Ensured rigidity and minimized run-out for precision machining.
    • Measurement: RENISHAW – Guaranteed accuracy and quality assurance throughout the process.

    Conclusion

    The "Candlelight Weaving Tower" is more than a competition result; it is a powerful demonstration of Taiwan's advanced manufacturing capabilities. It shows that with the right combination of innovative design, state-of-the-art machinery like the Victor Taichung Vcenter-AX380, and a collaborative ecosystem of technology partners, CNC machining can transcend its traditional role to become a true art form. As the competition continues to foster talent and push boundaries, we can expect to see even more remarkable creations that redefine the intersection of technology and art.
