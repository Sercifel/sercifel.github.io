MECT 2027 Guide: What to Know Before the Nagoya Machine Tool Exhibition

Oct 20, 2027

MECT 2027, officially known as MECHATRONICS TECHNOLOGY JAPAN 2027, will take place in Nagoya, Japan, from October 20 to October 23, 2027.

The exhibition covers a broad range of manufacturing equipment and technologies, including machine tools, cutting tools, measuring equipment, industrial robots, CAD/CAM/CAE, IoT, and AI.

For those planning to attend or looking for information about the event, this guide summarizes the key MECT 2027 details currently available from the official organizer.

What Is MECT 2027?

MECT is a machine tool exhibition that began in 1987 and is held in the autumn of odd-numbered years at Port Messe Nagoya. MECT 2027 will mark the 21st edition of the exhibition.

The previous event, MECT 2025, hosted 524 companies and organizations across 2,092 booths and recorded 77,613 visitors. For MECT 2027, the organizer currently projects approximately 2,000 booths and 70,000 visitors.

When and Where Will MECT 2027 Take Place?

MECT 2027 will run for four days at Port Messe Nagoya, using Exhibition Halls 1, 2, and 3.

  • Dates: October 20–23, 2027
  • Opening Hours: 10:00–17:00
  • Final Day: 10:00–16:00 on October 23
  • Venue: Port Messe Nagoya
  • Address: 2-2 Kinjofuto, Minato-ku, Nagoya, Aichi 455-0848, Japan

The event is organized by News Digest Publishing Co., Ltd., with the Aichi Machine Tool Merchants Association serving as co-organizer.

What Will Be Exhibited at MECT 2027?

The official exhibition scope extends beyond machine tools and includes manufacturing equipment, components, software, automation technologies, and related systems.

Major exhibition categories include:

  • Machine tools
  • Metal forming and sheet metal processing machines
  • Injection molding machines and 3D printers
  • Machine accessories and cutting tools
  • Measuring and testing equipment
  • Grinding wheels and abrasives
  • Hydraulic, pneumatic, and water hydraulic equipment
  • Gears and gear systems
  • CAD/CAM/CAE
  • Control systems and related software
  • IoT and AI technologies
  • Industrial robots and material handling equipment
  • Industrial cleaning equipment
  • Environmental and safety equipment
  • New materials
  • Micromachines and nanotechnology

The range of categories allows MECT to cover multiple areas of manufacturing, including machining, tooling, measurement, automation, production software, and supporting equipment.

How Much Does It Cost to Attend MECT 2027?

According to the official event information, general admission is JPY 1,000 per person.

For groups of 10 or more, admission is JPY 500 per person. Admission is free for:

  • People who complete advance registration
  • Overseas visitors
  • Students

Registration procedures and other visitor information may be updated as the exhibition approaches, so the official MECT website should be checked before attending.

How Can You Get to MECT 2027?

MECT 2027 will take place at Port Messe Nagoya in Kinjofuto, Nagoya.

For public transportation, the official event website provides access information using the Aonami Line from the Nagoya Station area.

Those traveling by car can use Kinjofuto Parking, which has capacity for 5,010 vehicles. The official access page also provides directions for those arriving via expressway.
