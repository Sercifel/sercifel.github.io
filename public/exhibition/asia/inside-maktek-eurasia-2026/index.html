Inside MAKTEK Eurasia 2026: Machinery and Manufacturing Technologies in Istanbul

Sep 28, 2026

MAKTEK Eurasia 2026 will take place from September 28 to October 3, 2026, at the Tüyap Fair and Congress Center in Istanbul, Türkiye. Held every two years, the exhibition focuses on machinery, metalworking, manufacturing technologies, industrial software, and related production systems.

For those considering attending the event, this guide covers the main exhibition categories, schedule, venue, and practical information available from the official MAKTEK Eurasia website.

What Is MAKTEK Eurasia 2026?

MAKTEK Eurasia is a machinery and manufacturing technologies exhibition held biennially in Istanbul. It brings together technologies used across different stages of industrial production, including machining, metal processing, tooling, measurement, automation, and manufacturing software.

For context, the previous edition in 2024 recorded 1,049 exhibitors and 87,000 visitors, with exhibitors representing 33 countries and visitors from 77 countries. These figures describe the 2024 event and should not be interpreted as projected attendance for 2026.

When and Where Is MAKTEK Eurasia 2026?

MAKTEK Eurasia 2026 is scheduled for six days:

  • Dates: September 28–October 3, 2026
  • Venue: Tüyap Fair and Congress Center
  • Location: Istanbul, Türkiye
  • September 28–October 2: 10:00–19:00
  • October 3: 10:00–18:00

Travel and accommodation information is also available through the official event website.

What Technologies Will Be Featured at MAKTEK Eurasia 2026?

The official scope of MAKTEK Eurasia includes several areas of machinery and manufacturing technology:

  • CNC machines
  • Cutting tools
  • Quality control devices
  • CAD, CAM, CAE, and PLM software
  • Robotic systems
  • Sheet metal processing technologies
  • Laser cutting machines
  • Bending machines

These categories cover different parts of the manufacturing process, from machining and metal forming to inspection, automation, and production software.

The official 2026 exhibitor directory is also available online, allowing companies and products to be reviewed before attending the event.

What Should You Know Before Attending MAKTEK Eurasia 2026?

Planning in advance can make it easier to navigate an exhibition covering multiple manufacturing categories. The official website provides an exhibitor list, product list, floor plan, ticket information, and venue details.

MAKTEK Eurasia also provides access to MyTüyap, Tüyap's digital fair application, where fair-related information for visitors and exhibitors is available on one platform.

According to the official ticket information, online tickets are valid throughout the six-day exhibition. Visitors should check the official website before traveling in case event information or entry arrangements are updated.

Before Visiting MAKTEK Eurasia 2026

MAKTEK Eurasia 2026 provides an opportunity to see machinery and manufacturing technologies from several related production fields at one event. Before attending, check the official MAKTEK Eurasia website for the latest exhibitor list, floor plan, ticket information, and any updates to the event schedule.
