From PLA to PHA: How Advanced Biodegradable Straw Making Technology Powers Global Sustainability & ESG Transformation

Jul 31, 2026

In an era where sustainability is no longer a corporate afterthought but a fundamental business imperative, the food and beverage (F&B) and packaging industries are undergoing a massive transformation. With single-use plastics banned or heavily restricted in over 100 countries—driven by stringent regulations such as the European Union’s Single-Use Plastics Directive (SUPD) and global ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) mandates—brands and manufacturers are racing to adopt genuinely eco-friendly alternatives.

At the forefront of this green revolution is the drinking straw, once a symbol of plastic waste, now evolving into a beacon of sustainable innovation. For manufacturers looking to capture this high-growth market, investing in advanced straw-making machinery capable of processing next-generation biodegradable materials is the ultimate strategic advantage.

As a trusted Taiwan-based manufacturer with nearly 40 years of engineering expertise, Jumbo Steel Machinery empowers global producers with state-of-the-art production lines designed for PLA, PHA, and other marine-degradable polymers.

The Material Evolution: Why PLA and PHA Are Reshaping the Market

Transitioning away from traditional Polypropylene (PP) requires robust, versatile extrusion technology. Today’s eco-conscious market demands materials that balance high performance with true environmental accountability:

  1. PLA (Polylactic Acid): Derived from plant-based starches such as cornstarch, PLA straws have become a mainstream staple for forward-thinking coffee shops, quick-service restaurants, and retail chains. They offer a rigid, crystal-clear appearance similar to conventional plastics while maintaining a significantly lower carbon footprint.
  2. PHA (Polyhydroxyalkanoates) & PHBH: Representing the pinnacle of marine-degradable innovation, PHA and PHBH naturally decompose in soil and marine environments without leaving microplastics behind. As global scrutiny on plastic waste intensifies, marine-degradable PHA straws are rapidly emerging as the gold standard for premium sustainable packaging.

Engineering Excellence: Inside Jumbo Steel’s PLA & PHA Straw Making Lines

Processing bio-based polymers requires precise temperature control, stable extrusion dynamics, and specialized screw designs to prevent material degradation and ensure consistent product quality. Jumbo Steel’s machinery—exemplified by advanced models like the JS 285/PHA Marine-Degradable Straw Making Machine and our high-efficiency PLA Straw Making Machines—is engineered to meet these exact industrial demands.

Key Technical Highlights of Jumbo Steel’s Solutions:

  • High-Speed & High-Output Performance: Designed for maximum productivity, our JS 285/PHA model achieves speeds of up to 800 pcs/min and extrusion outputs ranging from 35 to 60 kg/hr, ensuring optimal Return on Investment (ROI) for high-volume manufacturers.
  • Material Versatility: Our extrusion lines are engineered to process a wide spectrum of materials, including PP, PLA, PHA, PHBH, Cellulose Acetate, and natural fiber blends (bamboo and tea fiber), allowing manufacturers to adapt swiftly to changing market demands.
  • Precision Engineering & Durability: Built with high-quality, wear-resistant components, precision main screws (L/D ratio 30:1), integrated vacuum forming water tanks, and reliable cutting units, our machines guarantee long service life and minimal downtime.
